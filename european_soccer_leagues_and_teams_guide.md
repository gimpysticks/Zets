## 3. Continental & Global Championships (Like UEFA)

UEFA is one of the six continental governing confederations under FIFA. Every confederation hosts equivalent tournaments for national teams and club teams.

### Continental Confederations & Premier Competitions

| Confederation | Region Covered | Top National Team Tournament | Top Club Tournament |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UEFA** 🇪🇺 | Europe | **UEFA European Championship** *(Euros)* | **UEFA Champions League** |
| **CONMEBOL** 🌎 | South America | **Copa América** | **Copa Libertadores** |
| **CONCACAF** 🌎 | North & Central America, Caribbean | **CONCACAF Gold Cup** | **CONCACAF Champions Cup** |
| **CAF** 🌍 | Africa | **Africa Cup of Nations** *(AFCON)* | **CAF Champions League** |
| **AFC** 🌏 | Asia & Australia | **AFC Asian Cup** | **AFC Champions League Elite** |
| **OFC** 🌊 | Oceania | **OFC Nations Cup** | **OFC Men's Champions League** |

### Secondary & Tiered Continental Club Competitions
* **UEFA:**
  * UEFA Europa League *(Tier 2)*
  * UEFA Conference League *(Tier 3)*
  * UEFA Super Cup *(Annual match between Champions League and Europa League winners)*
* **CONMEBOL:**
  * Copa Sudamericana *(Tier 2)*
  * Recopa Sudamericana *(Super Cup between Libertadores and Sudamericana winners)*
* **CAF:**
  * CAF Confederation Cup *(Tier 2)*
  * CAF Super Cup
* **AFC:**
  * AFC Champions League Two *(Tier 2)*
  * AFC Challenge League *(Tier 3)*
* **CONCACAF:**
  * Leagues Cup *(North American club competition between MLS & Liga MX)*
  * CONCACAF Central American Cup / Caribbean Cup *(Regional qualifying tournaments)*

### Global & Intercontinental Tournaments
* **FIFA World Cup:** The premier quadrennial tournament for senior men's national teams worldwide.
* **FIFA Club World Cup:** A 32-team quadrennial global championship contested by top club champions from all six continental confederations.
* **FIFA Intercontinental Cup:** An annual tournament featuring the winners of each confederation's premier club competition.
* **Finalissima:** An intercontinental super cup match between the reigning UEFA European Champions and CONMEBOL Copa América champions.

## 5. How to Identify What Competition You Are Watching

When tuning into a match, use these quick heuristics to determine the competition type:

### 1. Domestic League Matches (Weekends & Midweek League Rounds)
* **What you see:** Two teams from the **same country** playing (e.g., *Liverpool vs. Arsenal* in England, or *Real Madrid vs. Valencia* in Spain).
* **Format:** Regular season points format (3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a loss). No extra time or penalty shootouts.

### 2. European Continental Tournaments (Midweek: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday afternoons ET)
* **What you see:** Two teams from **different countries** playing each other (e.g., *Bayern Munich [Germany] vs. Inter Milan [Italy]*, or *Real Madrid [Spain] vs. Manchester City [England]*).
* **Competitions:**
  * **UEFA Champions League (UCL):** Premier top-tier continental championship (Tuesday & Wednesday). Broadcast on **Paramount+ / CBS Sports**.
  * **UEFA Europa League (UEL):** Second-tier continental championship (Thursday). Broadcast on **Paramount+ / CBS Sports**.
  * **UEFA Conference League (UECL):** Third-tier continental championship (Thursday). Broadcast on **Paramount+ / CBS Sports**.

### 3. Domestic Cup Competitions (Midweek Knockouts & Finals)
* **What you see:** Teams from the **same country**, often featuring top-division clubs playing against lower-division clubs in single-elimination knockout rounds.
* **Key Examples:**
  * **England:** FA Cup (ESPN+), Carabao Cup / EFL Cup (Paramount+)
  * **Spain:** Copa del Rey (ESPN+)
  * **Germany:** DFB-Pokal (ESPN+)
  * **Italy:** Coppa Italia (Paramount+)
  * **France:** Coupe de France (FOX Sports)
