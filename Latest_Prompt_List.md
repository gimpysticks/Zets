# Prompt List

## Sheet 3: Gothic & Horror Prompts

* Mist-shrouded graveyards with leaning headstones
* Mausoleums with cracked marble doors
* Black-feathered ravens perched on crosses
* Pale moonlight casting elongated shadows
* Haunted mirrors with faint reflections
* Shattered porcelain dolls with missing eyes
* Phantom carriages drawn by skeletal horses
* Candles flickering in crypt corridors
* Coffins lined with faded satin
* Whispering voices carried by the wind

## Tattoo Prompts

* A single, perfect circle or square.
* Interlocking triangles.
* A minimalist mandala with clean lines.
* A continuous line forming an abstract shape.

### Nature Elements
* A small, stylized leaf (e.g., a monstera leaf outline, a simple oak leaf).
* A minimalist mountain range silhouette.
* A tiny moon crescent or a simple circle for a full moon.
* A single wave line.
* A simple star or cluster of dots representing stars.

### Symbols & Icons
* A clean heart outline.
* An anchor.
* A compass rose with basic lines.
* An infinity symbol.
* A simplified arrow.
* A tiny lightning bolt.
* A peace sign.

### Animals (Silhouettes)
* A small bird silhouette (e.g., a swallow or a generic bird in flight).
* A minimalist cat or dog outline.
* A tiny bee or butterfly outline.

### Objects & Concepts
* A simple coffee cup outline.
* A minimalist camera.
* A single musical note.
* A stylized eye.
* A crown outline.
