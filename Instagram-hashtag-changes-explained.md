# Daily Challenge Post Checklist

## BEFORE YOU POST (2 min)
- [ ] Photos picked: Carousel of 3-5 (not single)
- [ ] Best photo is first — that's what people see in feed
- [ ] Caption drafted with keywords in FIRST 2 lines
    - Example: "Covington storefront reflection at 7am - building across street mirrored. Day 12 for Monochrome May."

## CREATING THE POST (in Meta Business Suite)
- [ ] Created in Business Suite, not Instagram app (so it hits Facebook too)
- [ ] Both Instagram + Facebook checked at top
- [ ] Tagged host(s) ON the photo: Tap Tag People > Add host
- [ ] @mentioned host(s) in caption first line: "For @HostName Day 12"
- [ ] Challenge hashtags: 1-2 ONLY, at end (under the 5 cap)
    - Example: #MonochromeMay2026
- [ ] No generic tags: Remove #photography #nikon #blackandwhite #reels #viral
- [ ] Alt text added: Settings > Advanced > Write Alt Text (describe photo with keywords)
- [ ] Scheduled for SAME time daily: 7:30am OR 7:30pm Eastern

## RIGHT AFTER POSTING (5 min)
- [ ] Shared to Stories > Added @Mention sticker for host
- [ ] Story posted

## GROWTH LOOP (15 min after posting - this is what holds you over 1200)
- [ ] Went to challenge hashtag > Recent tab > Found 8-10 entries from TODAY
- [ ] Filtered for accounts 1000-1500 followers (your size)
- [ ] Left 1 real sentence on each (not "nice!" — mention something specific in their photo)
- [ ] Went to host's Day prompt post > Commented on 3-4 other entrants there

## END OF DAY (2 min)
- [ ] Replied to all comments on YOUR post
- [ ] Checked Tagged Photos tab on host's profile — is your entry there?
- [ ] Did NOT follow/unfollow anyone just for ratio
