> * **Kill "Artistic" Titles:** Avoid conceptual titles like *"The Duality of Man"* or simple descriptors like *"Cool Guitar"*. Use descriptive, searchable titles:

  2. **Specific Subject (6–8 tags):** creepy portrait, victorian lady, weird vintage art, gothic home decor, macabre aesthetic

### **Layer 1: Automate Asset Prep & Metadata (Start Here)**

Redbubble has no public API for direct programmatic uploads, and headless scraping scripts (like raw Selenium or Playwright scripts uploading hundreds of items) frequently trigger anti-bot bans.

Where you save dozens of hours without risking your store is automating **everything that happens before you hit the browser**: image scaling, border generation for die-cut stickers, and auto-generating optimized titles, tags, and descriptions into a single clean CSV.

#### **1\. Local Image Processing with ImageMagick (imagemagick)**

Instead of manually opening image editors to crop, resize, or add a clean white die-cut border for stickers:

> * Ensure your source artwork is high-res (ideally 4500×5400 px or larger at 300 DPI).  
> * You can run a one-line Bash loop across an entire folder of transparent PNGs to generate crisp, padded sticker cutouts:

`# Generate a white sticker border contour around transparent PNGs`  
`for img in raw/*.png; do`  
  `name=$(basename"$img" .png)`  
  `convert "$img" \( +clone -channel A -morphology EdgeOut Diamond:15 +channel -fill white -colorize 100% \) \`  
    `-compose DstOver -composite "stickers/${name}_sticker.png"`  
`done`

#### **2\. Local Python Metadata Generator (metadata.py)**

Instead of staring at a blank tag box for every design, you can maintain a simple local YAML/JSON config of your core themes (e.g., Victorian Gothic, Surreal Creatures, Vintage Guitars) and run a Python script that outputs a ready-to-use CSV.
